DQuestions — in this order

Q1 is the structural ask; everything else hangs off it. Get their recommendation first on each, then pin mechanism, timeline, and projected numbers.

  1. NM demand into our GAM auction the structural ask

    "We want your demand competing for our unsold inventory inside GAM network 10039233 — Prebid price-priority line items or Open Bidding. Which do you support, what's the setup lift, and what's the timeline?" Lead with Exhibit A as the proof of need. Without this, "more coverage" just means more NM-owned units and a deeper silo.

  2. Property audit — EP article pages their plan

    "What would you add or change on our article template today? You run 4 units while ~29 non-NM slots sit beside them. Should your demand compete in the sidebar, and what would you expect it to earn?" Also: give us the placement name↔ID key (gunstar/prometheus/eagle/video ↔ 4740/60165/8045/…).

  3. Forum enablement new inventory

    "Your system returns zero placements for the forum. What placements do you recommend for XenForo templates (thread, list, sidebar), what's the setup lift, and what RPM do you project — post-IVT-filter numbers, please?" We handle the forum ads.txt deployment.

  4. Video: Connatix attribution + the request bloat one question, not two

    "Is the sidebar Connatix player your deal, and is your 'video' placement (80% of our 28.5M requests, 7.9% fill, $0.75 eCPM) its bid slots? Where does that revenue report?" Then: their recommendation for video — fix, expand to in-article outstream, or replace with their player — with rev share and projected incremental Session RPM in writing.

  5. Scroll gate — negotiate, don't demand a/b it

    "Show us the data behind the >3-scroll threshold. We'd like to A/B a lower gate (or time-on-page fallback) on a traffic slice and compare eCPM × fill." Expect their defense: the gate protects viewability/IVT on our ~40% junk share — which is why the ask is a test, not removal.

  6. Yield plan to $3+ commitments

    "Blended eCPM is ~$1; Safari and in-app clear at $0.76–0.79. What specifically changes — floors, demand mix, identity for cookieless Safari (UID2/ID5/LiveRamp), APS participation — and what lift will you commit to watching for, on what dates?"

  7. Reporting access — turned on first baseline

    "Placement-level and device-level splits (impressions, bid rate, win rate, RPM), self-serve. This goes live before any changes so every projection above is measurable against a baseline."

  8. ads.txt ownership + master-file errors leverage

    "We found and fixed the stale file ourselves. (a) Fix your master: the jammed appnexus/insticator line and the malformed smartadserver TAG-ID. (b) Give us a process — hosted redirect or notification on every update — so it never drifts again. (c) Confirm 04-2025 is the current seller set."
