04 — The 30-day plan

What gets done, in order

Lightweight execution. No new content required, no editorial disruption. The goal is to normalize what's already there.

01
Full affiliate opportunity audit across Expedition Portal and XOVERLAND
Map every outbound merchant link. Flag affiliate program status, network, and commission range for each brand.
02
Prioritize and activate truck build pages first
Starting with the six highest-value builds identified above. Back-tag all existing outbound links where affiliate programs are available.
03
Enroll in affiliate programs for untagged brands
Most brands (WARN, Rigid, REDARC, ARB, ICON) are available on AvantLink — Overland's existing network. Application, enrollment, and link generation handled in full.
04
Build and maintain a merchant tracker
Running doc tracking every brand, their program status, commission rate, network, and next action. Becomes the foundation for ongoing affiliate management.
05
Extend to sponsored gear pages and existing editorial where relevant
Truck builds are the priority, but any sponsored content or gear roundups get audited in parallel.
06
End-of-month readout: revenue signal and phase two recommendation
30-day summary of what was activated, early click and revenue data, and a clear recommendation on whether and how to expand.
