01 — The picture today

Almost all of your revenue comes from one lever — and it's the entry-level version of it.

Today, essentially all monetization across the portfolio is display advertising, and it's running on a single-buyer setup with no competition for your inventory. Affiliate is effectively switched off — the only affiliate links that exist are years-old Amazon shortlinks and a near-dormant network account. Sponsorship reach is being given away for free. None of this is a content or audience problem. It's a collection problem.

02 — Fix this first
🚨
A technical issue can take display revenue to $0 — fix it before anything else.

A sharing plugin on Expedition Portal is writing a user identifier that combines ad data with personal data — a direct violation of Google's publisher policy. Google actively scans for this and can suspend the ad-serving account with no warning, which would zero out display revenue — your primary income — overnight. The fix is fast (remove or reconfigure the plugin), but it gates everything else in this document. A secondary issue: several ad units are currently failing to load entirely, earning $0 on every failed impression.

03 — The three levers, ranked

Where the revenue is, and where it should be

1 · Display biggest + fastest

Your ad stack feeds a single buyer — no header bidding, no competition for your inventory. Your traffic already qualifies for the premium networks the major outdoor publishers use, which run 10–15 buyers against every impression and add a video player that can run your own X Overland clips (video pays 5–10× a banner). On the same traffic, that's a realistic 2–2.5× lift — and these networks guarantee to beat your current revenue or let you walk, so the downside is floored. (Current display baseline is estimated at ~$15K/mo and should be confirmed against your ad dashboard — it's the one number this upside rests on.)

2 · Affiliate greenfield, two clean moves

Kill the dead inventory: an off-topic affiliate ad widget is burning 675,000 impressions on ski boots, gas masks and survival food — brands that appear nowhere in your content — and earning $0. Then fill the empty pages: 37 of your 40 top Expedition Portal pages and 180 of 330 videos have no affiliate links at all. The little money that exists is on tiny pages; the big pages are blank. Turn on a sitewide auto-affiliate layer for the backlog, then hand-place links on the top guides and videos.

3 · Sponsorship free reach → paid

REDARC (21.9M view exposure), CBI (21.8M), Equipt (18.1M) and their peers already appear across your properties for free. Flat-fee "presented by" packages turn that reach into premium revenue — and unlike display, sponsorship is immune to ad blockers and reads as editorial, not advertising, so it pays without compromising the brand.
