The core finding: two systems, no bridge

05

Building the bridge — how NM demand enters the GAM auction

Two standard mechanisms. NM already runs a Prebid-style wrapper (powerad.ai), so Option A reuses what they have — the winning bid gets routed through GAM to compete with direct-sold instead of running in parallel. Confirm on the call which NM supports for network 10039233.

Option A · recommended

Prebid line items (header bidding)

NM's wrapper sends bids into GAM via ~20 price-priority line items keyed on hb_pb price buckets. NM competes against direct-sold on CPM, client-side. Most control and transparency; reuses their existing auction.

Option B · GAM-native

Open Bidding (server-side)

Add NM as an Open Bidding yield partner in GAM's unified auction. Lighter page weight, GAM-managed, but requires NM to support OB and your GAM to have it enabled. Less granular reporting.

Division of labor — and the line not to cross

NM's job: bid adapter/config and their side of setup. Your GAM's job: authorize it — price-priority line items (or the OB partner) mapped onto your ad units, so there's one set of slots. If NM counter-offers a managed-service / MCM takeover: acceptable only scoped to unsold inventory, with direct-sold priority untouched and your independent admin + reporting access retained.

06

Migration sequence — safe order of operations

Order matters: don't touch AvantLink or forum expansion until NM is actually backstopping the auction, or you just create more empty slots.

    ads.txt rebuilt (270 records) and verified — done. Still open: Montana click-tracking post-mortem + sponsor make-good; Hankook "needs creatives" (upload or pause); forum ads.txt file before any forum enablement.

  2. Authorize NM in GAM — NM sets up, us to approve

    Prebid price-priority line items (Option A) or Open Bidding. Map NM's placements (get the name↔ID key) to the matching GAM ad units. This creates layer 2. Also on the table: A/B a lower scroll-gate threshold on a traffic slice — ask for the data behind >3, don't demand removal.

  3. Resolve video attribution, then consolidate — NM answers, us

    Confirm whether "video" placement = Connatix bid slots and where the player's revenue reports. Then gate/kill the request bloat with full information. Remove the 8 dead AdX line items either way.

  4. Verify the backstop — us

    Confirm NM now wins unsold impressions: post-flight article inventory backstops at market price instead of house fill; forum slots (once enabled) fill. Watch blank-rate and Session RPM.

  5. Reposition AvantLink to layer 3 — us

    Move from Network/20% run-of-network to House priority 16 or scheduled promo campaigns. Now the 20%→5% reduction means something, because NM sits beneath it.

  6. Prove it on Session RPM — us + NM reporting

    Placement/device-level reporting on first (baseline), then 2-week before/after or A/B per change. Net win = NM revenue gained − AvantLink/house revenue lost.

  7. Expand the video lane — NM + us

    Based on the Connatix audit: fix, expand (in-article outstream), or replace. Projected incremental Session RPM in writing; video fill + VCR reported separately. Instream with XOVERLAND cut-downs once the editing pipeline exists.
