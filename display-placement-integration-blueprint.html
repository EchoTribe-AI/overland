01

The core finding: two systems, no bridge

This is the reframe. Your target model assumes direct-sold → NM → AvantLink is one ranked waterfall. It isn't. GAM fills its slots and NM injects and fills a completely separate set of slots. Neither ever bids on the other's inventory — so GAM's unsold impressions have no paying backstop, and NM only earns on the few slots its own script renders.

04

The target: three real layers in one auction

Your intended model, made mechanical. The difference from today is that all three layers compete for the same GAM slots in priority order — instead of GAM and NM each owning a separate set of slots.

1

Direct-sold sponsorships GAM priority 4–8 · guaranteed

Montana, Continental, Nitto, Mohab, AEV, Hankook, Overland Expo, OJ house. Keep exactly as-is — highest value, served first. No change to priority.

Today: healthy, but over-pacing (Montana at 548%) front-loads flights and starves everything below.

2

NextMillennium — in-auction backstop price priority 12 · every unsold impression

NM demand competes for whatever direct-sold doesn't take, at real market price, across main site and forum. This is the layer that fills the Belly/Footer blanks and catches inventory when Montana completes.

Today: does not exist inside GAM — NM runs in its own silo. This is the one thing to build.

3

AvantLink 2026 — promotional layer house priority 16 / scheduled promo

Repurposed from a blind 20% remnant skim into a deliberate feature slot: affiliate promotions and media buys you choose to run. It fills only what NM declines, or runs as scheduled campaigns you control.

Today: Network priority 12, 20% run-of-network, ~$0 value (82 clicks / 292K imps). Reposition, don't just trim.

▶

Video — its own lane dedicated outstream / instream

A real sticky outstream player (owned-content instream on the roadmap), reported separately, replacing the in-banner "video" request bloat. New demand, not a reshuffle.

Today: no video ad unit exists; "video" = display-eligible demand in banner slots.

05

Building the bridge — how NM demand enters the GAM auction

There are two standard mechanisms. NM already runs a Prebid-style wrapper (powerad.ai), so Option A reuses what they have — the winning bid just gets routed through GAM to compete with direct-sold instead of running in parallel. Confirm on the call which NM supports for network 10039233.

Option A · recommended

Prebid line items (header bidding)

NM's wrapper sends bids into GAM via ~20 price-priority line items keyed on hb_pb price buckets. NM competes against direct-sold on CPM, client-side. Most control and transparency; reuses their existing auction.

Option B · GAM-native

Open Bidding (server-side)

Add NM as an Open Bidding yield partner in GAM's unified auction. Lighter page weight, GAM-managed, but requires NM to support OB and your GAM to have it enabled. Less granular reporting.

Division of labor

NM's job: provide the bid adapter/config and set up their side. Your GAM's job: authorize it — create the price-priority line items (or enable the OB partner), and map NM's gunstar / prometheus / eagle demand onto the corresponding GAM ad units so there's one set of slots, not two.

06

Migration sequence — safe order of operations

Order matters: don't touch AvantLink or the blanks until NM is actually backstopping the auction, or you just create more empty slots.

  1. Housekeeping now — us, this week

    Sync ads.txt to NM's 04-08-2025 version; fix Montana click tracking (0/933K) and pacing; upload or pause Hankook ("needs creatives"). No dependencies — do immediately.

  2. Authorize NM in GAM — NM sets up, us to approve

    Stand up Prebid price-priority line items (Option A) or the Open Bidding partner. Map gunstar/prometheus/eagle to the matching GAM ad units. This creates layer 2.

  3. Consolidate slots & kill dead weight — us

    Retire the separate NM-injected placements so demand flows through GAM slots. Remove the 8 dead AdX line items. Gate the in-banner "video" request (80% of requests, $0.75).

  4. Verify the backstop — us

    Confirm NM now wins unsold impressions: forum Belly/Footer blanks fill, article slots backstop when Montana completes. Watch blank-rate.

  5. Reposition AvantLink to layer 3 — us

    Move from Network/20% run-of-network to House priority 16 (or scheduled promo campaigns). Now drop the share to 5% — it finally means something because NM is beneath it.

  6. Prove it on Session RPM — us + NM reporting

    2-week before/after (or GAM A/B) on Session RPM, per the field-manual test design. Net win = NM revenue gained − AvantLink revenue lost.

  7. Launch the video lane — NM + us

    Sticky outstream pilot with projected incremental Session RPM in writing; video fill + VCR reported separately from day one.
