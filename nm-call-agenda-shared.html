Expedition Portal + Forum · Display Advertising

NextMillennium Meeting Agenda

Goal: review the current state of the account together and agree on NextMillennium's plan to improve coverage and yield across expeditionportal.com and forum.expeditionportal.com.

Prepared · Jul 30, 2026 Attendees · Kelly, Jake + NextMillennium team

1Where things stand

Verified on our side over the past week.

  • ads.txt updated Jul 26. The live file was an older version missing 56 seller records; we deployed the current 270-record file and reporting normalized within a day.
  • NextMillennium currently serves 4 units per article page (two in-article units plus two fixed-position units), alongside roughly 29 other ad slots served through our Google Ad Manager.
  • The forum has no NextMillennium placements today — it runs older GAM ad slots only, and several positions regularly go unfilled.
  • When a direct-sold campaign completes, unsold inventory has no paid backstop. Our Montana State flight finished this week and those positions fell to house ads. NM demand does not currently compete inside our GAM auction.
  • Recent yield: about $28/day at a blended eCPM of $0.90–1.13. Desktop Chrome performs well; Safari and in-app inventory clear notably lower.
  • A Connatix video player runs in our article sidebar. We'd like to understand how it connects to the NM partnership and where its revenue is reported.
  • The NM loader activates after several scroll events, which limits how often its auctions run on our traffic.

2Discussion topics

  1. Connecting NM demand to our GAM inventory

    Options for NM to compete for unsold impressions inside GAM network 10039233 (Prebid line items or Open Bidding), so completed direct campaigns hand off to paid demand. Setup steps and timeline.

  2. Placement recommendations for article pages

    NM's suggestions for coverage on the article template, including whether their demand should compete for sidebar positions, and expected performance. A reference key matching placement names to the on-page units would help our reporting.

  3. Forum enablement

    Recommended placements for the forum templates (thread, list, sidebar), setup requirements, and projected RPM. We'll deploy an ads.txt file for the forum subdomain as part of this.

  4. Video strategy

    How the Connatix player fits into the partnership and its revenue reporting; the high request volume on the video placement relative to its fill; and NM's recommendation for video going forward (current player, in-article outstream, or an alternative).

  5. Loader activation threshold

    Background on the scroll-based activation, and whether a test of a lower threshold or a time-on-page trigger makes sense for our traffic mix.

  6. Yield improvement plan

    Specific changes to floors, demand mix, and identity solutions (particularly for Safari), and what improvement to expect over what timeframe.

  7. Reporting access

    Self-serve placement-level and device-level reporting (impressions, bid rate, win rate, RPM) so we can track results against a baseline.

  8. ads.txt maintenance

    A process for keeping the file current going forward — notification or hosted option on each update — and confirmation of the current seller list.

3Outcomes we're looking for

  • An agreed path for NM demand to compete for our unsold GAM inventory, with a setup date
  • NM's written recommendations with projected impact for each item
  • Forum enablement scoped with a timeline
  • Clarity on the Connatix player and its revenue reporting
  • Agreement on a loader-threshold test
  • Reporting access enabled
  • An ads.txt update process in place
